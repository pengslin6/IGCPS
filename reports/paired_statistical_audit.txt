DA-TGT Conservative Paired Five-Seed Statistical Audit
======================================================

Audit date: 2026-08-31
Seeds: 11, 22, 33, 44, 55
Primary test: two-sided paired Student t test
Multiplicity: Holm correction across six metrics within each dataset
Interval: 95% Student-t confidence interval for the paired mean difference
Sensitivity test: two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test

Comparator rule
---------------
For each metric, the displayed comparator is the rerun baseline with the
highest observed five-seed mean. This post-run conservative mapping is reported
as a sensitivity analysis and is not a preregistered selection rule.

Limitations
-----------
- Five pairs provide limited power. The exact two-sided Wilcoxon p value is
  0.0625 even when DA-TGT is higher in all five pairs.
- The audit reruns only the strongest comparable adapters, not every model in
  the point-estimate tables.
- The temporal test tails had already been inspected during the broader
  revision cycle. Results are retrospective reproducibility estimates, not an
  untouched confirmatory test.
- The audit does not establish universal superiority across plants, protocols,
  graph schemas, or deployment hardware.

No raw data, per-seed CSV files, predictions, figures, checkpoints, or
manuscript files are redistributed in this repository.
