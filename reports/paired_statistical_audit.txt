DA-TGT Complete Five-Seed All-Model and Paired Statistical Audit
================================================================

Audit completion date: 2026-09-02
Seeds: 11, 22, 33, 44, 55
Coverage: DA-TGT plus nine baseline adapters on each of two datasets
Completed evaluations: 50 model--seed runs per dataset; 100 total
Primary test: two-sided paired Student t test
Multiplicity: Holm correction across six metrics within each dataset
Interval: 95% Student-t confidence interval for the paired mean difference
Sensitivity test: exact two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test

Comparison boundary
-------------------
The nine baseline adapters are GCN-AE, GAT-AE, VGAE, GraphSAGE-AE,
IIoT-GNN, EE-GCN, STGaAN, STCI, and DT-GNN. Every adapter receives the same
causally fused rows, chronological partitions, train-fitted scaling, graph,
class labels, and form of supervised reference-node readout. The comparison
therefore evaluates each encoder family inside one common task harness. It is
not a claim that every source paper natively solves exact-node localization.

Cells below are five-seed mean+/-sample standard deviation in percent.

Comparator rule
---------------
For each metric, the paired comparator is the baseline with the highest
observed five-seed mean in the complete table above. This post-run conservative
mapping is reported transparently and was not preregistered.

Limitations
-----------
- Five pairs provide limited nonparametric power. With five same-direction
  nonzero pairs, the exact two-sided Wilcoxon p value is 0.0625.
- The temporal test tails had already been inspected during the broader
  revision cycle. Results are retrospective reproducibility estimates, not an
  untouched confirmatory test.
- The common supervised root scorer evaluates adapter representations under
  this task and is not a native reproduction of every baseline source paper.
- The audit does not establish universal superiority across plants, protocols,
  graph schemas, or deployment hardware.

No raw data, per-seed CSV files, predictions, figures, checkpoints, or
manuscript files are redistributed in this repository.
